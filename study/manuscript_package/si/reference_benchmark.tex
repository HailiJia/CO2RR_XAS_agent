\subsection{Native quantities, broadening, and notebook reconciliation}
FDMNES mode assignments follow decks/output banners, not directory names. Reference logs identify revision 11 April 2025, radius 7~\AA, SCF and Perdew. Five native convolutions report Gamma\_hole 1.55~eV, Gamma\_max 15~eV, Ecent 30~eV, Elarg 30~eV; these differ from the ML cohort's archived logs. Their energy-dependent broadening is retained with its native conventions. CuO Green includes both saved site contributions; CuO fdm is the raw site-1 curve without completed final-state convolution. FEFF 9.6.4 uses omega and mu, includes its intrinsic lifetime/self-energy (header Gam\_ch 1.729~eV), COREHOLE RPA, SCF 7~\AA{} and FMS 9~\AA{}. No second lifetime convolution is applied.

The VASP arrays are trace/3 of the final imaginary dielectric tensor, excluding off-diagonals, for one designated Cu core-hole atom. The native energy is a core-excitation energy requiring photon calibration. Inputs record ICORELEVEL=2, CH\_LSPEC, and CH\_SIGMA=0.1~eV. The official CH\_SIGMA guide identifies Gaussian broadening but does not independently pin the sigma/FWHM interpretation of these executables. The PBE and GW-labelled PAW lineages differ; folder GW does not prove a GW/BSE calculation. Separate Cu calibration offsets are therefore used.

The original notebook uses Gaussian sigma 1~eV and exploratory offsets +120/+125~eV, with another displayed +124~eV route. The actual Cu\_GW.dat cell is reproduced and saved separately, but its lineage to the parsed OUTCAR remains unverified. The original Cu\_PBE.dat is empty; it is explicitly not substituted while claiming notebook reproduction. The verified OUTCAR PBE is a different identified input. A later cell reuses intensity variables, so a coherent multi-method notebook route cannot be recovered past the empty input. Reference-v2 retains all raw files and these limitations.

The primary is explicitly a native-data relative-intensity comparison: added Gaussian sigma/FWHM and Lorentzian HWHM/FWHM are zero. This removes v1's unsupported universal Lorentzian FWHM 1.55~eV; it does not assert that native CH\_SIGMA establishes adequate instrumental broadening. The remaining physical resolution is unknown. A separate additional Gaussian sigma 1~eV (FWHM $2\sqrt{2\ln2}\sigma=2.35482$~eV) diagnostic uses a positive normalized kernel. Native lifetime is preserved. No width is fitted. Finite-domain kernel mass is renormalized; uniform-grid interpolation is confined to native support. Raw, native, additionally broadened, and aligned arrays are saved. Unequal native final-state treatments preclude an unconditional accuracy ranking.

\subsection{Background, normalization, and alignment}
The first substantive negative calculated absorption arose at v1's local-pre-edge subtraction (not in raw CuO fdm); the latest supplied display reached about $-0.209$. Native experimental baseline/noise negatives are a separate raw-input issue. The local windows were not established as physical pre-edges for every native energy convention. They are removed in v2, rather than concealed by absolute values, clipping, or a restricted y axis. Raw/pre-normalization diagnostics are retained; min--max scaling is a declared relative-intensity transform, not a repaired physical edge jump.

Processing is native parsing/tensor or duplicate averaging, retained native broadening, optional separately declared positive Gaussian diagnostic, calibrated energy shift, supported interpolation, then the same final min--max rule for every calculation and Experiment. The extrema are evaluated on the identical 8980--9020~eV, 201-point grid. Flat, nonfinite, or insufficient traces fail explicitly. No extrapolation is allowed. Substantive negative calculated pre-normalization absorption fails; a $10^{-12}$ roundoff tolerance is explicit. No independently justified all-series edge-jump sensitivity is available. This reference normalization does not alter the ML archive's minimum-subtracted unit-area representation.

The sign is $E_{\rm display}=E_{\rm native}+\Delta E$. One offset is fitted on Cu for each method and transferred unchanged to both oxides. Bounds (eV) are Green $[-4,4]$, fdm $[-4,4]$, FEFF $[-15,-6]$, PBE $[105,135]$, GW $[110,145]$, based on photon-output convention, notebook-scale offsets, and distinct native core onsets. Every trial must support the full same comparison window. The objective is min--max RMSE, with a 0.05~eV profile and local bounded refinement. There is one fitted shift per method and no fitted widths. Separately fitted compound shifts within $\pm5$~eV of the transferable offset are shape diagnostics only. Boundary hits and RMSE+0.01 shift-sensitivity intervals are saved; these are not statistical confidence intervals. Native-axis views are blank where the full photon window is unsupported.

Cosine similarity is the normalized dot product without extra mean centering; Pearson correlates centered intensities; Spearman correlates average ranks with tie handling. Official SciPy cosine, pearsonr, and spearmanr implementations are used. Zero norms, constants, nonfinite arrays, and insufficient overlap yield unavailable metrics rather than zero agreement. Adjacent energy samples are correlated, so per-point correlation p-values are not reported. The identical final arrays feed Figure~3 and the tables. No comparable hardware/resource/problem-size timing logs establish a measured speedup.
